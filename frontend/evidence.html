Research contribution & next work

  1. Longitudinal prediction: train on current and prior records to estimate what was subsequently recorded.
  2. Leakage prevention: split patients, not individual visits, and fit preprocessing on training data only.
  3. Honest baselines: evaluate whether a learned model beats copying the current historical value.
  4. Explainability: feature attribution on the model, not clinical causation.
  5. Missing-data experiments: from-scratch reconstruction evaluated separately; synthetic imputations are not measured lab values.
  6. Future research: independent hospital validation, better minority-class labels, verified dose units, and prospective clinician-led outcome evaluation.

Academic and retrospective research only. Not a treatment, prescribing, or warfarin adjustment application.
